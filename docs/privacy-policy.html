Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This policy covers two iOS apps that are part of the open-source Network Traffic Monitor project (github.com/GreyWizardPublic/network-traffic-monitor):

  • NTM Dashboard — a companion viewer app that displays network traffic statistics from your self-hosted NTM server.
  • NTM Client — a network monitoring client that captures traffic flow metadata from your iOS device and forwards it to your self-hosted NTM server.

Neither app is operated as a service. Both apps connect exclusively to a server you own and configure. No data is sent to the developer or any third party.

Data we do not collect

Neither app collects, transmits, or shares any personal data with the developer or any third party. Specifically:

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are included.
  • No advertising identifiers are accessed.
  • No user accounts are created on any developer-operated server.

NTM Dashboard — data stored on your device

  • Server configuration (server URL, polling interval) — stored in iOS UserDefaults, never leaves your device except to connect to the server you configured.
  • Session token — after signing in with a passkey, an opaque Bearer token is stored in the iOS Keychain. It is sent only to the server you configured and is deleted when you sign out.
  • Pinned TLS certificate (optional) — stored in UserDefaults for certificate pinning against your private server.

NTM Client — packet tunnel and traffic data

NTM Client uses iOS's Network Extension framework (NEPacketTunnelProvider) to create a local VPN tunnel on your device. This tunnel is used solely to observe network traffic flow metadata — it does not route your traffic through any external server or third-party infrastructure.

  • What is captured: Flow metadata only — source/destination IP addresses, ports, protocol, and byte counts. Packet payloads (the actual content of your communications) are never read, stored, or transmitted.
  • Where it goes: Flow metadata is forwarded exclusively to the NTM server address you configure — a server you own and operate on your own network. It is never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • User consent: iOS displays a system VPN permission prompt before the tunnel can be activated. You must explicitly approve it. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → VPN & Device Management.
  • Server configuration (server address, passkey credentials) — stored in the iOS Keychain and UserDefaults on your device.

Network connections

Both apps connect exclusively to the server address you enter in Settings. Neither app makes connections to any other host. All connections use TLS (1.2 minimum).

Passkey / WebAuthn

Passkey registration and authentication are handled entirely by iOS and your configured server. No biometric data or passkey material is accessible to the apps — iOS manages passkeys in the Secure Enclave.

Children's privacy

These apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect information from children.
